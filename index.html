Loan programs

Purchase and Refinance

Each program below is written for either a purchase or a refinance, anywhere in California.

Conventional

Fixed and adjustable rate loans for borrowers with established credit and documented income. Down payment as low as 5%.

FHA

3.5% down payment and more forgiving credit score requirements.

VA

100% financing for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, reservists and surviving spouses.

Construction Loan to Permanent Loan

One-time close financing to build your new home.

Bridge

Short-term financing so you can close on the next home before the current one has sold.
