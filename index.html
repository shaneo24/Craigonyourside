Loan programs

Purchase and refinance

Every program below is written for either a purchase or a refinance, anywhere in California or Arizona.

Conventional

Fixed and adjustable-rate loans for borrowers with established credit and documented income. Conforming and high-balance limits.

FHA

A lower down payment and more forgiving credit requirements, for first-time buyers and anyone rebuilding.

VA

No down payment for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, reservists and surviving spouses.

Construction

Financing to build or substantially renovate, with funds released in draws as the work is completed.

Bridge

Short-term financing so you can close on the next home before the current one has sold.
